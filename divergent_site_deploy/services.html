Services & Pricing

What a custom build looks like

Clear scope, tested tools, and a handoff that leaves you able to run it yourself.

What we build

Custom tools for real businesses

Custom tools for real businesses and for people who need something specific built. Typical work:

  • Job, shift and pay tracking; quotes and invoicing; inventory and ordering.
  • Daily close-out, tips and labor reports from Clover, Square or Toast exports.
  • Bookkeeping helpers that read bank, card and payment CSVs and hand your accountant clean numbers.
  • Website data collectors and scrapers; chatbots and room utilities.
  • Desktop calculators, control panels and broadcast audio tools.
  • Short-term builds and fixed-scope pilots.

How a project runs

Four steps, no surprises

1. Scope

We write down what it does, what it does not do, and what "done" looks like.

2. Build

I design each system and test everything before it ships. You see progress in small steps.

3. Handoff

README, setup steps, tests and the working tool on your machine.

4. Support window

30 days of free fixes on anything Divergent Engines built, for issues that fall inside the agreed scope.

Pricing

Quoted once the scope is clear

Custom builds typically $1,500 to $5,000, quoted per project once the scope is clear. Larger shop systems are quoted in phases.

Standard hourly work is $100 per hour (varies with involvement). Repairs and fixes are $125 to $150 per hour with a one-hour minimum. Emergency and after-hours work is $200 per hour.

Every build includes 30 days of free fixes on anything Divergent Engines built.

Every quote names the deliverables, the timeline and what happens if the scope grows. No surprises, and nothing billed for work you did not agree to.
