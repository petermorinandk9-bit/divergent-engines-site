How a project runs

Four steps, no surprises

1. Plan

We go through what you need, one step at a time, identify the bottlenecks in your process, and plan the build around them. Quotes are usually returned within 24 hours once we have the details.

2. Build

I design each system and test everything before it ships. I send you progress updates as the project comes together.

3. Handoff

You receive the working software, setup instructions, and a walkthrough of how to use it. We help you get it running and confirm it works for the process we agreed on. Most builds are completed within one week, with the timeline agreed before work begins.

4. Support window

30 days free support on all Divergent Engines builds.
