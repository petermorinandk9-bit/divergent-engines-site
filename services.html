What a custom build looks like

A clear plan, tested tools, and a handoff that leaves you able to run it yourself.

What we build

Custom tools for real businesses

Custom tools for real businesses and for people who need something specific built. Typical work:

  • Job, shift and pay tracking; quotes and invoicing; inventory and ordering.
  • Daily close-out, tips and labor reports from Clover, Square or Toast exports.
  • Bookkeeping helpers that read bank, card and payment CSVs and hand your accountant clean numbers.
  • Website data collectors and scrapers; chatbots and room utilities.
  • Desktop calculators, control panels and broadcast audio tools.
  • Short-term builds and fixed pilots.

How a project runs

Four steps, no surprises

1. Plan

We write down what it does, what it does not do, and what "done" looks like.

2. Build

I design each system and test everything before it ships. You see progress in small steps.

3. Handoff

README, setup steps, tests and the working tool on your machine.

4. Support window

30 days of free fixes on all Divergent Engines builds, for issues that fall inside what we agreed.
