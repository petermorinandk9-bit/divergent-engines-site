How a project runs

Three steps, no surprises

1. Plan

We go through what you need, one step at a time, identify the bottlenecks in your process, and plan the build around them. Quotes are usually returned within 24 hours once we have the details.

2. Build

I design each system and test everything before it ships. I send you progress updates as the project comes together.

3. Handoff

You receive the working software, setup instructions, and a walkthrough. We help you get it running and confirm it works for your agreed process.
