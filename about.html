Divergent Engines builds custom software for people who run real businesses. Started late 2025 as an idea; first tool built in a single 30-hour stretch during a winter blackout on generator power. More than nine working tools since: job tracking and load tickets for timber and hauling operations, formulation calculators, data collection tools, audio software, an open-source database of ten Native American languages. Founder Peter Morin grew up in family businesses in the U.P. and has worked in the trades, on the road and on the water, so the tools fit how a shop actually runs. They work from data you already have, run on your own computer, and don't require changing the systems you use today.
